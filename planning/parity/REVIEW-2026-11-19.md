## What caused the Stormkeep differences

Crossing original and rebuilt brushes with original and rebuilt patch triangles isolated all six mismatches to compiled world brushes. Five have corresponding geometric changes in the editor map. The sixth retained exactly the same ten brush planes but changed its compiled contents from solid to translucent lava. The solid-only trace passed through it and hit the floor 16 units lower. The report identifies the responsible brushes, editor source and trace rows. With original map binaries, all 4,800 recorded traces match within tolerance under the compatibility settings. ## Multiplayer evidence

The paired recordings used separate graphical clients and servers over loopback on the same hashed Stormkeep BSP. Both games reached the frozen state and fired Crylink. Different viewpoints, bot behavior and impacts limited comparison of short projectile fades and individual buff pickups. The runs also exposed two observable native defects. Bots fired other weapons in a named Vortex-only arena, and firing Crylink with unlimited ammunition drove the cells display negative. Both defects were fixed. On a native repeat with matching settings, only Vortex appeared in the firing log. Crylink retained its infinity indicator and zero cells after two bursts and a server-confirmed self-frag. The capture report preserves the inputs, frame sheets, event logs and scope of each observation. [Visual comparison](paired/index.html) · [Paired sessions and post-fix evidence](paired/README.md)

On Running Man CTF, the initial native neutral shield obscured the control-point pad. The original uses an additive entity flag. The native renderer now honors that flag for the affected model, and a repeat from the same viewpoint showed the pad and scrolling text. Later paired recordings show construction, damage, repair, neutral reset and generator destruction. [Scripted objectives](scripted-objectives/README.md) [Onslaught frames and material evidence](paired-onslaught/README.md)

Debug and Release builds succeeded. All 5,971 tests in the integrated .NET 8 suite passed. Four additional checks replayed the two real original demos with the reference data supplied. Firefox verified the seven selected decisions and prompt export. The updated registry lists 615 ranked leads across 2,386 features, with 75 rows flagged for in-game verification.

The Astra and Sol reviews found edge cases in idle-bot barrier acknowledgment, server config commands, demo mounting, signon downloads and the timedemo clock. These were addressed in code. The final review also corrected Crylink’s initial fade delay check and tested both sides of the 255-tick boundary. A late observer can still receive a different fade offset, and native alpha updates follow the snapshot cadence. The recorded screenshots, automated tests and archive comparisons each establish a different part of behavior. Unverified observations remain in the [in-game queue](NEEDS-INGAME-CHECK.md).
